About the Service

Garbage Pickup Service for Calgary Homes

A recurring residential garbage pickup that works alongside your City collection.

How Garbage Pickup Works in Calgary

Most Calgary homes get City garbage collection every second week. That leaves a gap, and for many households the black bin is full long before the next collection. Our Garbage Pickup Service fills that gap. We collect your garbage on the weeks the City does not, so you get garbage pickup every single week.

Who Is This Service For?

Garbage Pickup Service is a residential service for Calgary homes. It suits larger families, busy households and anyone who regularly fills the black bin before collection day. Choose Standard, Plus or Extra depending on how many bags you usually have.

What Happens to Bigger Items?

Regular garbage pickup covers securely bagged household waste. For furniture, appliances, renovation debris or a full cleanout, use our junk removal service. You can check the full list of items we take or see junk removal pricing. Garbage Pickup Service customers can ask for a separate junk removal quote at any time.

Winter and Seasonal Help

The Junk Man also offers snow removal and holiday and permanent lighting, so one local crew can look after your home all year. Have a question? Contact us or call (403) 797-7145.

Frequently Asked Questions

Garbage Pickup in Calgary: Your Questions Answered

Still unsure? Call or text (403) 797-7145 and we will help.

What Is the Garbage Pickup Service?

It is a recurring residential garbage pickup for Calgary homes. The City of Calgary collects your black bin every second week, and The Junk Man collects on the weeks in between, so your garbage is picked up every week.

How Much Does Garbage Pickup Cost?

Garbage Pickup Service starts at $36 a month. Standard is $109 every 3 months, Plus is $139 every 3 months and Extra is $169 every 3 months, billed quarterly.

Is There a Contract?

No. There is no long-term contract.

Which Weeks Do You Collect?

We collect every second week, on the weeks the City of Calgary does not collect your black bin.

Do I Need to Be Home for Pickup?

No. You do not need to be home. Put your securely bagged and tied garbage out and we pick it up.

How Many Bags Are Included?

Standard includes up to 6 garbage bags per pickup, Plus up to 9 and Extra up to 12. Additional bags are available for an extra charge.

What Can Go in My Garbage Pickup?

Bagged household waste, food and kitchen garbage, packaging, non-recyclable household items and small miscellaneous garbage. Everything must be securely bagged and tied.

What Is Not Included?

Construction or renovation debris, concrete, bricks, dirt or sod, large furniture or mattresses, appliances and tires, paint, chemicals and hazardous materials, propane tanks, batteries and liquids, and excessively heavy bags.

What If I Have Something Bigger?

Garbage Pickup Service customers can contact us anytime for a separate junk removal quote for furniture, appliances, renovation debris and other large items.
