How our pricing works

Prices are a starting guide

Our advertised prices are standard guidelines. Your actual price can vary depending on:

  • Volume of material
  • Weight of materials
  • Type of materials being removed
  • Accessibility
  • Additional labour or dismantling requirements

Your quote is locked in

We give transparent, locked-in estimates based on your location and detailed photos. You get an accurate price before we arrive, with no unexpected charges for work already included in your quote.

How it works

Three steps. Zero hassle.

  1. Step 1

    Request your free quote

    Choose whichever option works best for you!

    By textSimply text us photos of the items you would like removed, along with your location. We will review everything and give you a personalized quote.
    OnlineUse our easy online booking system. Upload your photos, enter your location and job details, and select your estimated load size. We will review your submission and contact you promptly to confirm your quote.
  2. Step 2

    Receive your locked-in estimate

    We review your photos and job details to give you a transparent, personalized estimate based on the volume, weight, materials and labour involved.

    Once your estimate is confirmed and the job details stay the same, that is the price you pay. No surprises!

  3. Step 3

    Sit back and relax!

    Our friendly, professional team arrives at your scheduled time and takes care of all the heavy lifting, loading and cleanup.

    We handle the hauling and responsible disposal so you do not have to lift a finger!
