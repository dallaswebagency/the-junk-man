Quick answers

?

How much does junk removal cost?

Loads start at $79, and prices go up by truck size from there. Loading and cleanup are included. Send a few photos for an exact quote.

?

How fast can you come?

We offer same-day service when the schedule allows. Send a photo or call and we will give you the earliest time.

?

Is there anything you cannot take?

Hazardous waste is the one thing we cannot take. The City of Calgary has drop-off points for those items.

?

What happens to the items you collect?

We donate gently used furniture and household items, and we recycle what cannot be donated. We aim to donate or recycle over 60% of what we receive.
