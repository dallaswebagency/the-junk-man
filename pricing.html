Every job is different! Our pricing considers the volume, weight, type of materials, accessibility, and labour required.

Simply send us a few photos of your junk and your location, either online or by text, and we'll provide a transparent, upfront estimate before any work begins.

No surprises. No hidden fees. Just honest, straightforward pricing.

How Is Our Pricing Calculated?

Our pricing is based on how much space your items take up in our trailer, along with weight, type of materials, accessibility, and the labour required. We provide transparent, upfront estimates so you know what to expect before any work begins.

Will My Quoted Price Change?

No surprises here! As long as the items and job conditions match your original description, the price you're quoted is the price you pay. If any additional charges apply, we explain them upfront before work begins.

What Can Make My Price Higher?

Heavy materials, tires, electronics, paint, construction debris, condo or apartment removals, and jobs requiring extra sorting or labour may cost more. Pickups outside Calgary city limits may also include a travel charge.

Can I Save Money?

Yes! You can save up to 15% by having your junk gathered in an easy-to-access spot like your driveway, garage, or curbside. Less labour means more savings for you!

What Items Do You Not Take?

We cannot take hazardous waste such as chemicals, fuels, or asbestos. The City of Calgary has safe disposal options for these items. Not sure about something? Just ask us!

How Soon Can You Pick Up My Junk?

We offer flexible scheduling, including same-day and next-day junk removal, subject to availability.

SPECIALTY HANDLING & DISPOSAL

Certain items and job conditions may require additional charges due to disposal fees, weight, accessibility, or extra labour.

This may include tires, electronics, paint, appliances, construction debris, unusually heavy items, condo and apartment removals, or jobs involving numerous small items scattered throughout a property.

Additional labour charges may also apply to jobs requiring extensive sorting, gathering, dismantling, or moving items over longer distances.

No surprises! Any additional charges will be clearly explained and included in your upfront quote before work begins.
